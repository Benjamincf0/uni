Technical interview:
Restate the question in your words to confirm your understanding, don’t start coding right away.
Ask clarifying questions! 
Look at the bigger picture
Draw out images to visualize solution and explain your thought process
Explain why the naive solution doesnt work
If working in silence is how you process best, call it out, and then summarize your thought process once ready
Be nice & normal.

They value candidates that thrive w ambiguity.
Googlers tackle problems that are seldom clearly defined
The most logical solution isn’t necessarily the best one
Googlers analyze problems by taking into account all dimensions and layers. Not just the surface level
For you: ask questions and feel free to explore alternate approaches to what you’re solving.

Your CV needs a good narrative, tailored to the specific role and company you're applying for.
